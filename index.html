FAQs

Your questions, answered

Common questions about Fit Txt, your text-based accountability buddy.

What happens after I sign up?

You will get a welcome text at the mobile number you provide. From there, Fit Txt will learn your goals, schedule, diet or exercise plan, and preferred accountability style, then use text check-ins to help you follow through.

How does the free trial work?

You can try Fit Txt free for 14 days with no card required. Trial users get 10 replies per day. After 14 days, we will text you a link if you want to continue. Paid users get 25 replies per day. Early access users pay $29/month, and the future standard price will be $39/month.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Fit Txt works over SMS/MMS, so you can reply from your normal text messages. That's the whole point. No new app or program to learn. Fit Txt meets you where you already are.

Can I stop the messages?

Yes. Reply STOP at any time. You can also reply HELP for support details. If you have an active subscription, replying STOP will end all future charges and messages.

Is Fit Txt medical or personal training advice?

No. Fit Txt provides motivation and accountability support, not medical care, diagnosis, dietetic advice, therapy, or certified personal training. We are evaluating the possibility of providing professional support in the future.

Will a human read my messages?

Most replies are AI-powered, but paid users have access to a human accountability partner who can take over and provide high-touch support and accountability. Fit Txt admins may also review conversations to provide extra support, improve safety, and troubleshoot issues.
