FAQ

Questions before you try Fit Txt?

The short version: it is text-based accountability, built for people who want follow-up without downloading another app.

What happens after I sign up?

You will get a welcome text at the mobile number you provide. From there, Fit Txt will learn your goals, schedule, diet or exercise plan, and preferred accountability style, then use text check-ins to help you follow through.

Is Fit Txt free during beta?

Yes. The first beta group is free while we test the experience. Premium human accountability may become a paid option later, and we will be clear before anything is billed.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Fit Txt works over SMS/MMS, so you can reply from your normal text messages.

Can I stop the messages?

Yes. Reply STOP at any time. You can reply HELP for support details.

Is Fit Txt medical or personal training advice?

No. It provides motivation and accountability support, not medical care, diagnosis, dietetic advice, therapy, or certified personal training.

Will a human read my messages?

Most replies are AI-powered, but authorized Fit Txt admins may review conversations to provide support, improve safety, troubleshoot issues, or help with premium accountability.

Fit Txt is not medical care and does not replace a doctor, registered dietician, therapist, or certified personal trainer. For support, email help@fittxt.com.
