How It Works

Accountability by text, without another app to manage.

Fit Txt learns what you are working toward, checks in by SMS, and pushes you to follow through with the level of directness you choose.

1

Join the beta

Enter your name, mobile number, and optional email. You will need to agree to receive recurring Fit Txt SMS/MMS messages before we text you.

2

Get your welcome text

After opt-in, Fit Txt can send a welcome text and begin learning about your goals, schedule, diet or workout plan, motivation style, and any restrictions you share.

3

Set the accountability level

Some people want friendly encouragement. Others want blunt coach-style pressure. Fit Txt is designed to personalize the tone so the push feels useful, not random.

4

Check in by text

Most conversations happen with AI over SMS. Fit Txt can ask about workouts, meals, weigh-ins, missed plans, and next actions so your goals stay in front of you.

5

Add human accountability when available

Premium support may include a real human accountability partner alongside AI. That person is there for follow-up and motivation, not certified medical, nutrition, or personal training advice.

Premium Accountability

Fit Txt starts with AI-powered accountability, but Premium is designed for people who want a real human accountability partner alongside the AI. Premium users get higher message limits and can receive additional human follow-up when extra support would help.

The human role is accountability, motivation, and follow-through. Premium does not mean you are receiving certified medical, dietetic, therapy, or personal training advice.

How We Keep Data Safe

Fit Txt only asks for information that helps provide accountability. Conversations may be stored so the service can remember context, show history to authorized admins, and provide support. Admin access is permission-based, and we do not sell SMS opt-in data or consent information to third parties for their own marketing.

We use trusted service providers for messaging, payments, hosting, and AI responses. Those vendors process information as needed to operate Fit Txt.

Text Message Controls

  • STOP: unsubscribe from Fit Txt text messages at any time.
  • HELP: get help or support information by text.
  • START: restart messages if your carrier supports it, or contact support@fittxt.com if you need help opting back in.

Important Health Note

Fit Txt provides accountability support, not medical care. It does not replace a doctor, registered dietician, therapist, or certified personal trainer. If you have a medical concern, injury, emergency, or feel unsafe, contact a qualified professional or emergency services.
